ABOUT

MORE THAN ONE THING.

I'm Aasif — a student, creator, and experimental web developer.

I'm currently studying Class 11 Humanities, while exploring different creative and technical worlds. I build websites, experiment with interfaces, edit gaming videos and photos, photograph the sky, explore AI, and write stories that often lean toward the darker side.

I'm still learning — and that's intentional.

I work with CapCut regularly while learning tools like After Effects, Photoshop, and DaVinci Resolve. On the web, I'm interested not only in making things work, but in how they look, feel, and make people experience something.

Sometimes I build something useful.

Sometimes I build something strange.

Sometimes I build something just to see what happens.

I don't have everything figured out yet.

Maybe that's the interesting part.
